# Andoya Spaceport

A launch site for Kitten Space Agency at Andoya in northern Norway. It adds "Andoya Spaceport" to the launch site list of the vehicle editor; no stock site is changed. Made for KSA build 2026.11.10.5801.

- One launch pad on a quay with a flame trench to the sea, an access tower, light masts, pipe racks, a tank farm, containers and a hall.
- Lamps light the pad at night and fade in when the sun sinks below 8 degrees.
- The site casts sun shadows when the vessel shadows are on in the graphics settings.
- A kitten can walk the tower stairs and the open flame trench.
- Keep legs, fins and boosters within about 15 m of the vehicle axis; a base wider than about 28 m does not fit the pad.

The lamps and the shadows need StarMap. Without it the site exists, but stays dark and has no shadows.

To remove the mod, delete the folder `AndoyaSpaceport`.
